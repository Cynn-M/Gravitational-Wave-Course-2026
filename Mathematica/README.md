## Projects

### EOB Hamiltonian and Gravitational-Wave Radiation Reaction

This project explores the effective-one-body (EOB) formalism and the incorporation of gravitational-wave emission into the dynamics of a two-body system.

The notebook covers:

- Expansion and construction of the effective EOB Hamiltonian.
- Canonical transformations between effective and real phase-space variables.
- Determination of the unknown Hamiltonian coefficients through post-Newtonian matching.
- Derivation of orbital quantities for eccentric Keplerian trajectories.
- Computation of gravitational-wave energy and angular momentum fluxes using the quadrupole formalism.
- Orbital averaging and verification of the energy and angular momentum balance laws against the Peters–Mathews results.

**Tools:** Wolfram Mathematica.
